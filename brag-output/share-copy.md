## LinkedIn
Most AI tools fail in commerce because language models hallucinate arithmetic.

When auditing millions of dollars in carrier freight bills, an off-by-one error or floating-point rounding glitch creates legal liability and cash burn.

We built Clawback to enforce an unbreakable architectural boundary:
1. AMD ROCm + Gemma extracts contract tariff rules, accessorial fees, and fuel surcharge matrices with verbatim section quotes.
2. A deterministic Python Decimal rules engine executes the audit calculations. Zero arithmetic is delegated to LLMs.

In our audited benchmark against 1,250 real-world freight lines:
- 284 overcharges recovered
- $9,130.51 recovered value
- $0.00 discrepancy against ground truth
- 3-column traceable proof for human clerk sign-off

Built for the AMD Developer Hackathon: ACT III (Track 3: Reinvent Commerce).
